# Independent reviewer instructions (substitute for the missing Part C)

You are an experienced CVPR area-chair-level reviewer with expertise in medical image analysis, correspondence/registration and general computer vision. You have not seen this paper before. Review the main paper PDF (and consult the supplement PDF only where the main paper points to it).

Read the PDFs with the Read tool (use the `pages` parameter, e.g. "1-6" then "7-11"; the supplement may need several calls). Do not search the web. Do not look at any other files.

Write your review as markdown with these sections:

1. **Summary** (3-5 sentences): problem, method, main evidence.
2. **Strengths** (bullets).
3. **Weaknesses** (bullets, most important first). Be specific: cite section, figure, table, equation or page.
4. **Clarity and presentation**: writing, notation, math readability, figure and table quality (name any figure/table that is weak and say why).
5. **Questions for the authors** (up to 6).
6. **Most impactful fixable changes** (up to 5, ranked): changes the authors could make with existing results and writing alone (no new experiments), and separately up to 3 that would need new experiments.
7. **Scores**:
   - Overall score on a 1-10 scale, where 1 = strong reject, 3 = reject, 5 = borderline, 6 = weak accept, 7 = accept, 8 = strong accept, 10 = award quality. Give a point estimate and a plausible range.
   - Sub-scores 1-10: novelty, technical soundness, experimental evidence, clarity, figures and tables.
   - Confidence 1-5.

Judge the paper on its merits for CVPR. Be candid and calibrated; do not inflate or deflate. Note that this is an expanded working draft that may exceed the page limit; do not penalize length itself, but do penalize padding or redundancy.
